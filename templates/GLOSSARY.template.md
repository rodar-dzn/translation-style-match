# Glossary

Every proper noun and coined term, with its binding rendering and the
evidence for it.

**Status values**

- `CANON` — found in the reference corpus. Binding. Cite where you found it.
- `NEW` — the corpus is silent; coined here. Record which CANON entries the
  formation was patterned on.
- `OPEN` — unresolved. Raise with the user rather than guessing.

**The rule:** verify before you invent. Most apparent gaps are terms the
corpus does render, under a phrasing you did not predict. Search plausible
variants — including inflected and compound forms — before writing `NEW`.

The **Reject** column feeds the linter: list every wrong variant you have
seen or expect, comma-separated. Near misses matter most — a rendering that
differs by one letter reads as a typo to a returning reader.
